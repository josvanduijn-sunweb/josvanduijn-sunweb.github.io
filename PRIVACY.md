# Privacy Policy for My Stuff Counter

Last updated: September 22, 2026

My Stuff Counter does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The counters you create are saved only on your device and never leave it. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

If you delete the app, all of its data is removed from your device.

Questions? Contact mystuffcounter.support@gmail.com.
