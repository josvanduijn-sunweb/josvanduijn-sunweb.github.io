❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new counter?
Tap the add button on the main list screen, give your item a name, and it'll appear in your list ready to count.
How do I increase or decrease a count?
Open any item from your list and use the + and − buttons to adjust its count. Changes are saved automatically.
Can I rename or delete a counter?
Yes — swipe on an item in your list, or open it and use the edit options, to rename, reset, or delete it.
